🛡️ Prem Milan Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Prem Milan has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or any form of child endangerment.

1. Age Requirement

Prem Milan is intended only for adults aged 18 years or older. Users below the age of 18 are not permitted to create an account or use our services.

2. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Prem Milan strictly prohibits:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Child sexual exploitation
  • Grooming or attempting to exploit minors
  • Any sexual or abusive content involving minors
  • Any activity that violates child protection laws

3. Reporting and Blocking

Users can report or block any profile that violates our community standards.

  • 🚩 Report User feature
  • 🚫 Block User feature
  • Admin review of reported accounts
  • Immediate action against violating accounts

4. Enforcement

If any account is found sharing illegal content or engaging in child exploitation, Prem Milan may:

  • Permanently suspend or terminate the account
  • Remove illegal content immediately
  • Preserve evidence where legally required
  • Cooperate with law enforcement authorities

5. Community Safety

We encourage all users to maintain respectful communication and immediately report suspicious or illegal activity.

6. Compliance

Prem Milan complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
